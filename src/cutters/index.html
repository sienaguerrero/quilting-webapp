Clay Quilt Cutters

Cookie cutters, but for quilt blocks in clay. Every shape comes in three block sizes, and every cutter comes with a pusher, so nothing stays stuck inside.

All 30 shapes

Drawn to scale from the same OpenSCAD file that makes the STLs. Sizes are for the clay piece, not the cutter.

Printing notes

  • Cutters stand 15 mm tall. The top 6 mm thins to a 0.8 mm blade, and a 4 mm flange around the base gives you something to press on.
  • Pushers are 0.6 mm smaller than their cutter all the way around, with a 3 mm plate and a knob to hold.
  • The hexagon, 60° diamond, and equilateral triangle share a side length, so they mix in one layout.

Other sizes

This page has STLs for 60, 75, and 100 mm blocks. For any other size, change block in quilt_block_cutters.scad, or run the export script from the zip. ./export.sh 90 3 makes 90 mm blocks with 3 Log Cabin rounds. It needs OpenSCAD installed.

Below about 55 mm the Dresden blade gets too narrow for a pusher knob, and the export stops on that piece.
